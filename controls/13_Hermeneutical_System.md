13_Hermeneutical_System
Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF)

Version: 0.1.0
Status: PROPOSED. Not canonical. Not adopted.
Classification: Draft for Human Engineer review
Date: 2026-11-18
Scope: How NBBF reads Scripture when it designs, tests, reviews, or
defends a budget, a tax, a spending Node, or a fiscal rule.

Lineage:
This draft restates the founder's hermeneutical system for the NBBF
domain. The same system is restated for NBLF (AGCL) as the proposed
amendment to AGCL 000 — Scripture Chief Control, version 0.2, and will be
restated separately for NBEF. This draft creates no CONTROL authority.
It becomes authoritative only if the Human Engineer authorizes it through
an NBBF-CWC and the Root (CONTROL 00) recognizes CONTROL 13.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

Every budget is a claim on someone's property. This CONTROL sets how
Scripture is read to decide whether a claim is lawful, bounded, and fair.

It is a Strong Meat plan (Hebrews 5:12-14), not a church plan. Its
subject is the structure of civil budgets under Christ the King.

==================================================
2. CHIEF PREMISE
==================================================

Every nation has Christ as King. Whether a nation enjoys Christ as King
depends on whether its government system adheres to the Regulative
Principles of Liberty.

==================================================
3. ORDER OF THE TEXTS
==================================================

Tier 1 — King James Bible (KJB). Read first, every time. Chosen for the
highest level of English grammar and vocabulary, not because it is the
best translation.

Tier 2 — Original Texts (Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek). For deeper issues
only. The original meaning in the original language may overrule the
KJB translation. Every overrule is a recorded Ruling that names the
word, its original meaning, and the source.

Reference — Geneva Bible notes (1560-1599). A historical witness to how
Reformation and Puritan readers understood civil authority. See
research/Puritan-Mind-1560-1776.md.

==================================================
4. THE REGULATIVE PRINCIPLES OF LIBERTY, APPLIED TO BUDGETS
==================================================

Every NBBF budget, tax, and spending Node must pass all six.

RP1 — God makes the rules.
Rulers are ministers, not owners (Romans 13:4).
Budget meaning: every appropriation traces to a written, enumerated
authority. The Human Owner of a Node is a steward of the money, not its
owner.

RP2 — What is authorized is permitted; what is not authorized is
forbidden to government.
"Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye
diminish ought from it" (Deuteronomy 4:2), by analogy.
Budget meaning: no tax, fee, or spending line exists unless written
authority grants it. Silence in the authority is a no.

RP3 — No elite capture.
Adding to the rule is drift (Genesis 3:3; Matthew 15:9).
Budget meaning: no off-ledger additions, discretionary assessments, or
insider carve-outs beyond what the written authority prescribes.

RP4 — Rain on the just and the unjust.
"He maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain
on the just and on the unjust" (Matthew 5:45).
"The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less than
half a shekel" (Exodus 30:15), Analogy: a sanctuary offering,
applied here to civil levies.
"Thou shalt not have in thy bag divers weights, a great and a small"
(Deuteronomy 25:13), Command.
Budget meaning: the same rule for every payer and every recipient. No
means testing, no favored class, no hidden measure.

RP5 — Enumerate, limit, protect.
"Thou shalt not steal" (Exodus 20:15). "Proclaim liberty throughout all
the land" (Leviticus 25:10), with the Jubilee reset.
"For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first,
and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?"
(Luke 14:28), Principle.
"The borrower is servant to the lender" (Proverbs 22:7), Principle.
Budget meaning: every Node declares its purpose, payer, cost, duration,
and sunset before it spends. It protects life, liberty, and property,
and it does not bind the next generation with debt it did not consent
to.

RP6 — Remember.
"That the generation to come might know them... and not forget the works
of God" (Psalm 78:6-7). "The children of Ephraim, being armed, and
carrying bows, turned back in the day of battle... And forgat his works"
(Psalm 78:9-11).
Budget meaning: every reauthorization reviews what past budgets of the
same kind actually produced, read through Scripture, and the record is
kept so the next generation can learn from it.

==================================================
5. WITNESSES
==================================================

Economists, historians, and theologians are witnesses who agree, never
authorities. The economists are witnesses to results: they confirm that
a budget rule produces results in the real world. A witness never
changes a rule alone.

==================================================
6. METHOD FOR A BUDGET RULING
==================================================

0. State the budget question: what claim, tax, or spending is tested.
1. Read the whole story in the KJB, with what comes before and after.
2. Compare Scripture with Scripture.
3. Name every counter-text. For taxes this includes Romans 13:6-7
   ("For this cause pay ye tribute also"): taxation is legitimate but
   bounded, and it must show why it continues.
3a. If the English is disputed or carries the Ruling, consult the
   Original Texts. Record the word, its meaning, and the source.
4. Classify the link: Command, Principle, Precedent, Analogy, or Modern
   design.
4a. Test against RP1 to RP6 (Section 4).
5a. Call the witnesses: do the economists confirm it produces results?
5. State the Ruling in one sentence, with its date and version.
6. Record what would change the Ruling.

For AI assistants: use the KJB only through step 4a. Go to step 3a and
step 5a only when the Human Engineer asks or the question is a Ruling.

==================================================
7. SCOPE LIMITS
==================================================

This CONTROL governs how Scripture is read for NBBF budget questions. It
does not own Root authority, Signal operations, property/pooling,
Exit/Disengagement, review calendar, cost/value, audit/performance, or
any other CONTROL's subject. It does not bind anyone's conscience or
church membership. Correction is by lawful process only.
